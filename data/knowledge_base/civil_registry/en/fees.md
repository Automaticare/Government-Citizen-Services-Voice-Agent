# Fees — Civil Registry Services (2026)

## General Fee Schedule

| Service | Channel | Fee (TRY) |
|---|---|---|
| Birth registration | Civil Registry / Hospital | **Free** |
| Birth certificate copy (paper) | Civil Registry Directorate | 95 TRY |
| Birth certificate copy (e-Government) | Online | **Free** |
| Certificate of residence | Civil Registry Directorate | 75 TRY |
| Certificate of residence (e-Government) | Online | **Free** |
| Civil registry extract (individual) | Civil Registry Directorate | 75 TRY |
| Civil registry extract (e-Government) | Online | **Free** |
| Marriage application fee (nikah harcı) | Municipality / Civil Registry | 850 TRY |
| Marriage certificate (official ceremony) | Municipality | Included in marriage fee |
| Marriage certificate copy | Civil Registry Directorate | 95 TRY |
| Address change registration | Civil Registry Directorate | **Free** |
| Address change (e-Government) | Online | **Free** |
| International birth certificate (multilingual) | Civil Registry Directorate | 150 TRY |
| Apostille endorsement (provincial directorate) | Provincial Civil Registry | 300 TRY |

---

## Payment Methods

- **Cash** — at the cashier desk of the civil registry directorate
- **Debit/credit card** — at the POS terminal in the directorate
- **Online (e-Government)** — card payment or e-Government payment system
- **PTT** — fee payment at a tax office or PTT (post office) branch

---

## Important Notes

- Documents obtained through e-Government carry a barcode and digital signature and are officially valid.
- Fee amounts are updated annually in line with the revaluation rate under the **Fees Law (Harçlar Kanunu)**.
- Citizens receiving social assistance (holders of green card or social support certificates) may be **exempt** from certain fees; they must apply to the directorate with the relevant documents.
- Fees paid for applications that are rejected due to incorrect or missing information are **non-refundable**.
- For the current fee schedule, visit the **General Directorate of Civil Registration and Citizenship Affairs (NVI)** website: [nvi.gov.tr](https://www.nvi.gov.tr)
