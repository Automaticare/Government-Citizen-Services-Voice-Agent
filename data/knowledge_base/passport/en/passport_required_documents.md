# Required Documents for Passport Application

## New Passport Application
- Original national ID card (Turkish Republic identity card)
- 2 biometric photos (taken within the last 6 months, 5x6 cm)
- Passport fee payment receipt
- Parental consent form for applicants under 18

## Passport Renewal
- Current passport (expired or about to expire)
- Original national ID card
- 2 biometric photos
- Passport fee payment receipt
- If name changed on passport: court order or marriage certificate

## Lost/Stolen Passport
- Lost/stolen declaration form (available at civil registry offices)
- Original national ID card
- 2 biometric photos
- Passport fee payment receipt
- Police report if applicable (in case of theft)

## Important Notes
- Biometric photos must be front-facing with a white background
- Applications must be made in person; power of attorney is not accepted
- For children under 18, both parents must be present at application
- Expedited passport requests are subject to additional fees
