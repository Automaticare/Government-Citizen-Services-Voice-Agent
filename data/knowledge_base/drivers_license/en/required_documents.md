# Driver's License Transactions - Required Documents

## New License Application

### Mandatory Documents
- National ID card or Turkish Republic identity card (original and photocopy)
- Proof of residence (issued within the last 6 months; obtainable via e-Government portal)
- Medical report (from an approved driving school physician or public hospital)
- Criminal record certificate (obtainable via e-Government portal)
- 2 biometric photographs (taken within the last 6 months, white background)
- Driving school enrollment certificate and course completion certificate
- Theoretical and practical driving exam pass documents

### Medical Report Requirements
- Eye examination result (visual acuity certificate)
- General health examination
- If required: hearing examination, psychiatric evaluation report

---

## License Renewal

### Mandatory Documents
- Current driver's license (original)
- National ID card (original and photocopy)
- Medical report (if validity period has expired or if requested)
- 2 biometric photographs
- Address confirmation document (if address has changed)

### Notes
- Mandatory renewal is required every 10 years.
- Drivers aged 60 and above must provide a medical report every 5 years.
- Renewal applications can be submitted no earlier than 6 months before the expiry date.

---

## Lost / Stolen License

### Mandatory Documents
- National ID card (original and photocopy)
- Loss or theft declaration document (official statement obtained from the relevant police station)
- 2 biometric photographs
- Petition letter (addressed to the Traffic Registration Office, with wet ink signature)

### Important Notes
- In case of theft, a police report must be filed and an official statement obtained.
- Publishing a newspaper announcement for a lost document is not mandatory.
- If lost abroad, application can be submitted through the Turkish consulate.

---

## License Class Upgrade

### Additional Documents
- Current driver's license (original)
- Driving school certificate for the relevant class
- Medical report (in accordance with requirements for the new class)
- Psychotechnical examination certificate (required for classes above Class B)
