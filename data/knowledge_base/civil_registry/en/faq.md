# Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ) — Civil Registry Services

## Birth Certificate

**Q: How long do I have to register my child's birth?**
A: For hospital births, the hospital automatically submits the notification. However, if the family needs to apply to the civil registry directorate, registration must be completed **within 30 days of the birth**. Late registration may result in administrative proceedings.

**Q: Can I get a birth certificate through e-Government?**
A: Yes. On the e-Government Gateway (turkiye.gov.tr), search for "Birth Certificate" (Doğum Belgesi) to instantly download a free, barcoded, and digitally signed document. This document is accepted by official institutions.

**Q: Can a birth be registered without a father's record?**
A: Yes. For births outside of marriage, the child can be registered solely under the mother's surname based on her declaration. The father's record can be added later through a court decision or the father's official declaration.

## Certificate of Residence

**Q: How long is a certificate of residence valid?**
A: There is no statutory expiry date, but most institutions require a document obtained within the **last 3–6 months**. Documents downloaded from e-Government include the issue date.

**Q: I live in a different city — do I have to go to my registered district's directorate?**
A: No. A certificate of residence can be obtained from **any civil registry directorate** in Turkey or via e-Government. As long as your registered address is up to date in the MERNİS system, you can get it from the directorate in the city where you currently are.

**Q: Can I get a certificate of residence without updating my address?**
A: Yes, you can obtain a document reflecting your currently registered address. However, if your actual address differs from your registered address, it is recommended to update your address first.

## Marriage

**Q: How far in advance do I need to make an appointment for a marriage application?**
A: Depending on demand — particularly during summer months — it is advisable to make an appointment **at least 4–8 weeks** in advance. Appointments can be made via e-randevu.nvi.gov.tr or the relevant municipality's system.

**Q: I got married abroad — do I need to register it in Turkey?**
A: Yes. Marriages that took place abroad must be registered either through the Turkish consulate or by **applying to a civil registry directorate** upon return to Turkey. An apostilled foreign marriage document and its Turkish translation are required.

**Q: Is a medical report required for marriage?**
A: Although it was abolished in 2014, some municipalities still request a medical report. It is recommended to contact the relevant institution in advance to confirm their requirements.

**Q: I lost my marriage certificate — how can I get a new one?**
A: You can obtain a **copy of the marriage certificate** by applying to the district civil registry directorate with your National ID card. The fee is **95 TRY** per the 2026 schedule.

## General

**Q: Which services can I access through e-Government without visiting a directorate?**
A: Birth certificates, certificates of residence, civil registry extracts, address change notifications, and family status documents can all be obtained free of charge through e-Government.

**Q: How can I reach the call centre?**
A: You can reach the General Directorate of Civil Registration and Citizenship Affairs call centre by dialling **ALO 199** (weekdays 08:30–17:30).
