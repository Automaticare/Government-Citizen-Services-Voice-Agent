# Driver's License Transactions - Application Process

## New License Application - Step by Step

### 1. Enroll in a Driving School
- Apply to and register at an authorized driving school.
- You will be asked for your ID and medical report at the time of registration.
- After completing the theoretical training, you will take the theoretical exam.

### 2. Theoretical Exam
- The exam is conducted on computer at centers approved by the Ministry of National Education.
- It consists of 50 questions; a score of 70 or above is considered a pass.
- Exam appointments can be made through your driving school or via the e-Government portal.

### 3. Practical Driving Training and Exam
- Practical driving training begins after passing the theoretical exam.
- The driving exam is administered by the provincial examination board.
- Documents are collected after successfully passing the exam.

### 4. Application at the Traffic Registration Office
- Visit the Traffic Registration Office with all required documents.
- Documents are reviewed; a photograph and signature are taken.
- The license is delivered to your address or available for pickup at the office within 7-10 business days.

---

## License Renewal - Step by Step

### 1. Book an Appointment
- Book an appointment via e-Government or by calling the ALO Interior Affairs line at 182.
- Walk-in queuing at the office is also possible without an appointment.

### 2. Obtain a Medical Report (If Required)
- A new report is required if 10 years have passed since the last license was issued.
- It can be obtained from your family physician or a full-service public hospital.

### 3. Apply at the Office
- Go to the relevant Traffic Registration Office with your documents.
- Pay the fee at the office cashier or in advance via e-Government.
- The renewed license is delivered to your address within 7-10 business days.

---

## Lost / Stolen License - Step by Step

### 1. File a Report
- In case of theft, go to the nearest police station and obtain an official statement.
- In case of loss, submitting a petition is sufficient.

### 2. Prepare Your Documents
- Prepare your ID, photographs, and the police statement.
- Write your petition addressed to the Traffic Registration Office.

### 3. Apply at the Office
- Apply at the Traffic Registration Office with your documents.
- You may request a temporary driver's license if needed.
- The new license is delivered within 7-10 business days.

## Processing Timeframes

| Transaction | Average Duration |
|---|---|
| New license (including training) | 2 - 4 months |
| License renewal | 10 - 15 business days |
| Lost license re-issuance | 7 - 10 business days |
| License class upgrade | 1 - 3 months |
