# Office Locations and Information - Istanbul

## Overview

The offices listed below handle civil registry, identity, passport, and related administrative services across Istanbul's districts. Appointments can be booked at [turkiye.gov.tr](https://www.turkiye.gov.tr) or by calling ALO 181.

---

## Kadikoy District Civil Registry Office

- **Address:** Moda Cad. No:22, 34710 Kadikoy / Istanbul
- **Phone:** (0216) 336 XX XX
- **Transport:** Metro M4 – Kadikoy station (5-min walk); Ferry terminal (10-min walk)
- **Parking:** Paid open-air car park near Moda market area.
- **Services Offered:** National ID card, passport, address change, birth/death registration, civil registry records

---

## Uskudar District Civil Registry Office

- **Address:** Hakimiyeti Milliye Cad. No:8, 34672 Uskudar / Istanbul
- **Phone:** (0216) 553 XX XX
- **Transport:** Ferry terminal (3-min walk); Metrobus – Uskudar stop
- **Parking:** Municipal car park opposite the ferry terminal (hourly rate).
- **Services Offered:** National ID card, passport, residence certificate, family registry records, death certificate

---

## Besiktas District Civil Registry Office

- **Address:** Barbaros Bul. No:47, 34353 Besiktas / Istanbul
- **Phone:** (0212) 261 XX XX
- **Transport:** Bus lines 22, 22RE, 25E; Ferry terminal (8-min walk)
- **Parking:** Akmerkez or Besiktas ferry car park available (paid).
- **Services Offered:** National ID card, passport, marriage/divorce registration, residence certificate

---

## Bakirkoy District Civil Registry Office

- **Address:** Zuhuratbaba Mah. Tevfik Ileri Cad. No:5, 34142 Bakirkoy / Istanbul
- **Phone:** (0212) 543 XX XX
- **Transport:** Metro M1A – Bakirkoy station (7-min walk); Suburban train – Bakirkoy stop
- **Parking:** Free 1-hour parking at the municipal car park adjacent to the office building.
- **Services Offered:** National ID card, passport, military registry, family census records, address change
