# Driver's License Transactions - Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

---

**Q: When does my driver's license need to be renewed?**  
A: Driver's licenses are valid for 10 years from the date of issue. For drivers aged 60 and above, this period is 5 years. You can apply for renewal up to 6 months before the expiry date.

---

**Q: Can I apply for a driver's license through e-Government?**  
A: Appointment booking, fee payment, and document inquiries can all be handled via the e-Government portal (turkiye.gov.tr). However, you must visit the Traffic Registration Office in person for document submission and photograph capture.

---

**Q: I lost my driver's license. What should I do?**  
A: First, go to the nearest police station to report the loss and obtain an official statement. Then visit the Traffic Registration Office with your national ID, 2 photographs, and a written petition. The re-issuance fee is 1,100 TRY.

---

**Q: Is a foreign driver's license valid in Turkey?**  
A: Licenses from countries that have signed agreements with Turkey are valid for use in Turkey for a limited period and under certain conditions. If you are a long-term resident, you are required to convert your license to a Turkish driver's license.

---

**Q: Can I drive abroad with a Turkish driver's license?**  
A: Turkish driver's licenses are valid in many countries under international agreements Turkey has joined. It is recommended to obtain an International Driving Permit (IDP) for driving abroad. Applications are made at the Traffic Registration Office; the fee is 700 TRY.

---

**Q: Where can I get a medical report?**  
A: You can obtain a medical report from your family physician, a physician affiliated with your driving school, or the outpatient clinics of full-service public hospitals. The report may include additional examinations depending on the license class.

---

**Q: Is an appointment required for driver's license transactions?**  
A: An appointment is not mandatory; however, it is recommended to book one via e-Government or by calling 182 to reduce waiting time. Walk-in service at the office is also available.

---

**Q: How can I reset my driver's license penalty points?**  
A: To reset driver's license penalty points, you must complete a traffic safety training course. This training is provided by institutions approved by the General Directorate of Security. For details, visit emniyet.gov.tr/trafik.

---

**Q: At what age can I obtain a Class B driver's license?**  
A: A Class B (passenger car) license can be obtained at age 18. Class A1 (motorcycle) is available at age 16, Class A2 at 18, and Class A at 24.

---

**Q: What happens if I fail the theoretical exam?**  
A: If you fail the exam, you may retake it once the waiting period has passed. The retake fee is 350 TRY. After two consecutive failures, completing additional theoretical training is mandatory before retaking the exam.

---

**Q: Are there special provisions for citizens with disabilities?**  
A: Yes. Depending on the nature of the disability, vehicle adaptation and special licensing conditions are available. No document fee is charged for driver's license transactions for individuals with disabilities. Contact Guidance and Research Centers or Traffic Registration Offices for further information.
