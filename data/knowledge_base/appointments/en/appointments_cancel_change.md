# Cancelling or Changing an Appointment

## General Rules

- It is recommended to cancel or reschedule your appointment **at least 24 hours in advance**.
- Last-minute cancellations create unnecessary load on the system and prevent other citizens from booking available slots.
- Failing to attend without prior cancellation results in a "no-show" record, which may temporarily restrict your ability to book future appointments.

---

## Cancel / Change via e-Government Portal

1. Log in at [turkiye.gov.tr](https://www.turkiye.gov.tr).
2. Go to the "Randevularim" (My Appointments) section.
3. Select your existing appointment.
4. Click **"Iptal Et"** (Cancel) or **"Degistir"** (Change).
5. If rescheduling, select a new date and time and confirm; you will receive an SMS notification.

---

## Cancel / Change via ALO 181

- Call **181** and select the "Appointment cancellation / change" option.
- Have your Turkish ID Number and appointment reference number ready.
- Inform the operator of your cancellation or new preferences.

---

## Cancel via SMS or Email Link

- Use the **"Iptal Et" (Cancel)** link in your confirmation SMS or email for one-click cancellation.
- This method does not require a login but only supports cancellation — rescheduling must be done through the portal or by phone.

---

## Cancellation / Change Policy

| Situation                                | Outcome                                              |
|------------------------------------------|------------------------------------------------------|
| Cancelled 24+ hours in advance           | Slot freed; no restrictions applied                  |
| Cancelled less than 24 hours before      | Record kept; rebooking is still permitted            |
| No-show without cancellation             | No-show flag added; 30-day booking restriction       |
| Third no-show                            | 90-day restriction and notification to office manager|

---

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: How many times can I reschedule the same appointment?**
A: You may reschedule the same service up to **2 times within the same calendar month**.

**Q: Can I book a new appointment immediately after cancelling?**
A: Yes. The system updates in real time, so new slots become available immediately after cancellation.

**Q: I lost my confirmation message — where can I find my reference number?**
A: Check the "Randevularim" (My Appointments) section on your e-Government account, or call 181 for assistance.
