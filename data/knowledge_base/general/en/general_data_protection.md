# Data Protection Rights (KVKK)

## What Is KVKK?

The Law on the Protection of Personal Data (KVKK), Law No. 6698, entered into force in 2016. It is Turkey's primary data protection legislation, similar in scope to the EU's GDPR. This law protects your rights regarding how your personal data is collected, processed, and stored by public and private entities.

## Your Core Rights

| Right | Description |
|---|---|
| Right to information | Learn whether your personal data is being processed |
| Right of access | Obtain access to your data and request a copy |
| Right to rectification | Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete data |
| Right to erasure (right to be forgotten) | Request deletion of your data under certain conditions |
| Right to restriction | Request that processing of your data be suspended |
| Right to object | Object to data processing, including automated decisions |
| Right to compensation | Claim damages resulting from unlawful data processing |

## How to Submit a Request

### Direct Application to the Data Controller
1. Submit a written application to the institution holding your data.
2. Include your **National ID Number (TC)**, contact details, and a clear description of your request.
3. Accepted submission methods:
   - In person with a handwritten-signed petition
   - Via KEP (Registered Electronic Mail)
   - Through the e-Government Portal (turkiye.gov.tr)
   - By email with a secure electronic signature

### Response Timeline
- The institution must respond within **30 days**.
- If the request is complex, this may be extended to **60 days**; you must be notified of the extension and the reason.

## Filing a Complaint with the KVKK Authority

If you do not receive a satisfactory response:

- **Personal Data Protection Authority (KVKK)** — kvkk.gov.tr
- Application must be filed within **30 days** of the institution's response
- The process is free of charge and can be completed online

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Can I find out where my data is stored?**
Yes. You can submit an access request to the data controller to obtain this information.

**Can my erasure request be refused?**
Yes. Requests may be refused if there is a legal obligation, public interest, or ongoing legal dispute — but the reason must be communicated to you.

**Can I stop my data from being used for marketing?**
Yes. You may object to commercial data processing at any time and withdraw any previously given consent.
