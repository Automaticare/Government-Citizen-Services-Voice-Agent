# Appeal Rights

## What Is an Appeal?

An appeal is the legal right to challenge a decision, action, or complaint outcome issued by a public institution. Every citizen may appeal against administrative acts that affect them.

## Appeal Deadlines

| Appeal Type | Deadline |
|---|---|
| Administrative appeal (to institution) | **30 days** from notification |
| Administrative court action | **60 days** from notification |
| Application to the Ombudsman | Within 6 months |
| Council of State (Danistay) application | 30 days from first-instance ruling |

> **Important:** Missing deadlines may result in the loss of your right to appeal. Exercise your rights on time.

## Appeal Channels

### 1. Direct Appeal to the Institution
- Submit a written application to the authority that issued the decision.
- Must be filed within **30 days** of notification.
- The institution is obliged to respond within **30 days**.

### 2. Application to a Superior Authority
- You may apply to the relevant ministry or higher administrative body.
- Submit a reasoned petition along with supporting documents.

### 3. Administrative Court
- You may file an annulment or full remedy case in administrative courts.
- Legal representation is not mandatory, but recommended.
- The deadline to file is **60 days** from notification of the decision.

### 4. Office of the Ombudsman (Kamu Denetçiliği Kurumu)
- Can be approached before going to court.
- Free and faster alternative dispute resolution.
- Apply at: ombudsman.gov.tr or call ALO 182

## What to Include in an Appeal Petition

- Full name and National ID Number (TC)
- Date and reference number of the contested decision
- Grounds for appeal (legal or factual arguments)
- The outcome you are requesting
- Supporting documents (if any)
- Signature and date

## Legal Assistance

- **Bar Association Legal Aid Offices** — Free legal advice for eligible citizens
- **Ministry of Justice Legal Aid** — For citizens with limited financial means
- **e-Government Portal** — Track your application and access documents online
