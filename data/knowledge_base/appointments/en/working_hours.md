## Lunch Break

- Offices do not accept visitors between **12:30 – 13:30**.
- Any appointment slots in this window are automatically shifted to the slot immediately before or after.

## Peak Times and Expected Wait Times

### Busiest Periods
- **Monday mornings (09:00–11:00):** Start-of-week backlog leads to longer wait times.
- **2–3 working days before and after public holidays:** Demand for ID cards and passports spikes.
- **School enrollment season (August–September):** Address updates and civil registry requests increase significantly.

### Quietest Periods
- **Tuesday and Wednesday afternoons (13:30–15:00):** Typically the shortest wait times of the week.
- **Friday late afternoon (15:30–16:30):** Weekly foot traffic tends to be low.

## ALO 181 Availability

- **Automated system:** Available 24/7 (appointment booking, cancellation, status inquiry)
- **Live operator:** Monday–Friday, 08:00–20:00

---

## Important Notes

- On public holidays **no offices** provide in-person services; for emergencies, call the provincial civil registry on-duty line.
- On the eve of public holidays (arife), offices typically close at **13:00**.
- During summer (June–August) when schools are out, demand for passports and ID cards rises sharply — book your appointment well in advance.
