# Required Documents — Civil Registry Services

## Birth Certificate

### Hospital Births
- **Birth report** issued by the hospital (automatically forwarded to the civil registry directorate)
- **National ID card** of both mother and father
- **Marriage certificate** if the parents are married
- **Notarized power of attorney** if the father is abroad

### Non-Hospital Births
- **Report from an authorized healthcare professional** or witness statement
- **National ID card** of both parents
- **Proof of residence**
- For births outside of marriage: **written declaration** by the mother

---

## Certificate of Residence (Yerleşim Yeri Belgesi)

- Valid **Turkish National ID card** (with MERNİS-registered address)
- If address update is required: **rental contract**, **title deed**, or **utility bill** (within the last 3 months)
- For applications via e-Government: only a **Turkish ID number** and **e-Government password** are required
- For applications on behalf of another person: **power of attorney** and the applicant's **National ID card**

---

## Marriage Certificate (Marriage Registration / Evlilik Cüzdanı)

### Documents Required at Application Stage
- **National ID card** of both parties
- **Civil registry extract** for both parties (issued within the last 6 months)
- **Medical report** confirming no health conditions preventing marriage (still required by some municipalities)
- **2 biometric photographs each** (taken within the last 6 months)
- If either party was previously married: **finalized divorce decree** or **death certificate**
- For a foreign spouse: **passport**, **equivalency certificate**, and **apostilled civil registry record**

### On the Day of the Wedding Ceremony
- All of the above documents plus **2 official witnesses** (with their National ID cards)

---

## General Notes

- All documents must be submitted as originals along with photocopies.
- Documents in foreign languages must be translated into Turkish by a **sworn translator**.
- It is recommended to contact the civil registry directorate in advance to confirm document requirements.
- Many documents can be obtained online through **e-Government (turkiye.gov.tr)**.
