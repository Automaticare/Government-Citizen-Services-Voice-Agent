# Application Process — Civil Registry Services

## Birth Registration

### Hospital Birth
1. The hospital electronically forwards the birth notification to the civil registry directorate within **10 business days** of the birth.
2. The mother or father visits the **district civil registry directorate** with their ID document.
3. The officer enters the information into the system; the birth certificate is issued the same day.
4. Optionally, the birth certificate can also be downloaded via e-Government.

### Non-Hospital Birth
1. The family must apply in person to the civil registry directorate **within 30 days** at most.
2. A healthcare professional's report or a witness statement must be presented.
3. The officer reviews the documents; the registration is approved.

## Obtaining a Certificate of Residence

### Via e-Government (Recommended)
1. Go to [e-Government Gateway](https://www.turkiye.gov.tr).
2. Log in with your Turkish ID number and e-Government password.
3. Type **"Certificate of Residence"** (Yerleşim Yeri Belgesi) in the search box.
4. Click "Query and Download"; the PDF document is generated instantly.
5. The document carries a barcode and digital signature and is officially valid.

### In Person at the Civil Registry Directorate
1. Go to the district civil registry directorate with your ID document.
2. Take a queue number (appointment optional; can be made at **e-randevu.nvi.gov.tr**).
3. State your request to the officer; the document is usually ready within **10–15 minutes**.
4. Pay the fee receipt (75 TRY, 2026 schedule).

## Marriage Application and Wedding Ceremony

### Step 1 — Pre-Application
- The couple visits the **district civil registry directorate** or the **municipality marriage registration unit**.
- Documents are submitted and the marriage declaration form is signed.
- Appointments can be made via **e-randevu.nvi.gov.tr** or the relevant municipality's website.

### Step 2 — Document Review
- The institution reviews the documents; additional time is given for missing or incorrect documents.
- Approval typically takes **3–5 business days**.

### Step 3 — Wedding Ceremony
- On the approved date and time, the couple and **2 witnesses** attend together.
- The marriage registrar officiates the ceremony; the **marriage certificate (evlenme cüzdanı)** is signed.
- The marriage record is entered into the MERNİS system.
