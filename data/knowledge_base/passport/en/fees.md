# Passport Fees and Charges

## Standard Passport Fees (2026)

| Passport Type | Validity | Fee |
|---------------|----------|-----|
| 6-month passport | 6 months | 1,250 TL |
| 1-year passport | 1 year | 2,000 TL |
| 2-year passport | 2 years | 3,150 TL |
| 3-year passport | 3 years | 4,500 TL |
| 10-year passport | 10 years | 5,750 TL |

## Expedited Processing Fee
- Express processing: +1,500 TL (delivery within 1-3 business days)

## Payment Methods
- Cash payment at tax office
- Bank transfer (to the relevant tax office account)
- Online payment via e-Government portal
- Credit card online payment (installment options available)

## Exemptions
- Diplomatic and service passports are exempt from fees
- Reduced rates apply for citizens with disabilities
- Reduced rates available for citizens over 65

## Important Notes
- Fees may be updated annually
- The fee is determined based on the current rate at the date of application
- Fees are non-refundable if the application is rejected
