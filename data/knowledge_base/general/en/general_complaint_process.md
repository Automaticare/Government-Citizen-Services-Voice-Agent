# Complaint Process

## How to File a Complaint

Citizens can submit complaints through the following channels:

- **ALO 181** — Call center (24/7 service)
- **e-Government Portal** — Online application via turkiye.gov.tr
- **CİMER** — Presidential Communication Center (cimer.gov.tr)
- **In-person** — At the relevant institution's service points
- **KEP (Registered Electronic Mail)** — Official digital correspondence

## Information Required for a Complaint

| Field | Description |
|---|---|
| National ID Number (TC) | Required |
| Contact details | Phone number or email address |
| Subject of complaint | Clearly and specifically described |
| Supporting documents | Attach if available |
| Date and location | When and where the incident occurred |

## How Your Complaint Is Processed

1. Upon submission, you will receive a **reference number**.
2. The complaint is forwarded to the relevant department.
3. An authorized officer begins the review.
4. Additional information or documents may be requested if necessary.

## Response Timelines

| Application Type | Legal Deadline |
|---|---|
| General complaint | 30 days |
| Urgent applications | 7 business days |
| CİMER applications | 15 days |
| Right-to-information requests | 15 business days (extendable to 30) |

## After the Process

- The outcome will be communicated through your chosen contact channel.
- If the response is unsatisfactory, you have the **right to appeal**.
- Appeals must be submitted within **30 days** of the decision.
- You may also apply to the Office of the Ombudsman (Kamu Denetçiliği Kurumu).

## Important Notes

- Providing false information may result in legal liability.
- Anonymous complaints may not be processed.
- Filing the same complaint with multiple institutions simultaneously may delay resolution.
