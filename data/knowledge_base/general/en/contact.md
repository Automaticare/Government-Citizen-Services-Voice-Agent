# Contact Information

## Call Center Lines

| Line | Service | Hours |
|---|---|---|
| **ALO 181** | General Citizen Services Hotline | 24/7 |
| **ALO 182** | Office of the Ombudsman | Weekdays 09:00–17:00 |
| **ALO 183** | Social Support Hotline | 24/7 |
| **ALO 184** | Ministry of Health Hotline | 24/7 |
| **ALO 199** | Disaster and Emergency Management | 24/7 |

> Via ALO 181 you can file complaints, submit information requests, book appointments, and get referrals.

## e-Government Portal

- **Website:** turkiye.gov.tr
- **Mobile app:** e-Devlet (available on iOS and Android)
- Requires an e-Government password, mobile signature, or electronic signature to access services.
- Services available through the portal:
  - Document queries and downloads
  - Appointment booking
  - Application status tracking
  - Complaint and right-to-information submissions
  - Tax and Social Security (SGK) transactions

## CİMER — Presidential Communication Center

- **Website:** cimer.gov.tr
- **Phone:** 150
- Used for complaints, suggestions, and requests addressed to the Presidency.
- Response time: 15 days

## Physical Service Points

### Civil Registration Offices (Nüfus Müdürlükleri)
- Located in every district.
- Appointment booking: turkiye.gov.tr or ALO 181
- Services: national ID, residence address changes, family record requests

### PTT Post Offices
- Document delivery and official correspondence services
- Website: ptt.gov.tr

### District Governorates (Kaymakamlık) and Provincial Governorates (Valilik)
- Administrative applications at the provincial and district level
- Opening hours: Weekdays 08:30–17:30

### e-Government Kiosks
- Located in shopping centres, PTT branches, and public buildings
- Access selected e-Government services without a password

## Useful Tips

- Phone lines may be busy during peak hours (09:00–11:00 and 14:00–16:00).
- Using the e-Government portal for applications is usually faster than other channels.
- A valid government-issued photo ID is required for all in-person visits.
