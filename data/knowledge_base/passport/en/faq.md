# Passport Frequently Asked Questions

## General Questions

**Q: When does my passport expire?**
A: Check the "Valid Until" date on the last page of your passport. You can also verify via the e-Government portal.

**Q: Can I renew my passport before it expires?**
A: Yes, you can apply for renewal up to 6 months before the expiration date.

**Q: I want to get a passport for my child. What do I need?**
A: Both parents must be present for applications for children under 18. If only one parent is applying, a notarized consent from the other parent is required.

**Q: I lost my passport. What should I do?**
A: Visit your nearest civil registry office and file a lost declaration. In case of theft, file a police report first. Then apply for a new passport.

**Q: I want to change the name on my passport?**
A: A name change requires a court order. After the court decision is finalized, apply at the civil registry office. For surname change due to marriage, a marriage certificate is sufficient.

## Processing and Delivery

**Q: How long does it take to get a passport?**
A: Standard processing takes 5-10 business days. Express processing takes 1-3 business days (additional fee applies).

**Q: Can I receive my passport by mail?**
A: No, the passport must be collected in person or via notarized power of attorney.

## Fees

**Q: How much does a passport cost?**
A: The 2026 fee for a 10-year passport is 5,750 TL. See the Fees page for the full schedule.

**Q: If my application is rejected, will I get a refund?**
A: No, processing fees are non-refundable.
