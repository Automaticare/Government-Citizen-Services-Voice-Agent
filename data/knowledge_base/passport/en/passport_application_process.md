# Passport Application Process

## Step-by-Step Guide

### 1. Book an Appointment
- Book via e-Government portal or ALO 181 hotline
- Walk-in applications are not accepted
- Nearest available date is typically within 3-5 business days

### 2. Prepare Documents
- Gather all required documents (see Required Documents)
- Get biometric photos taken
- Pay the processing fee and keep the receipt

### 3. Visit the Application Office
- Arrive at the civil registry office at your appointment time
- Bring original copies of all documents
- Photocopies can be made at the office (for a fee)

### 4. Biometric Data Collection
- Fingerprint scan
- Digital photo capture
- Signature sample

### 5. Application Confirmation and Tracking
- A reference number is provided upon completion
- Track application status via e-Government portal
- SMS notifications are sent for status updates

## Processing Times
- Standard processing: 5-10 business days
- Express processing: 1-3 business days (additional fee)
- Processing times may be longer during peak periods (summer, pre-holiday)

## Collection
- Passport is collected from the application office
- ID must be presented at collection
- Collection by proxy is possible (notarized power of attorney required)
