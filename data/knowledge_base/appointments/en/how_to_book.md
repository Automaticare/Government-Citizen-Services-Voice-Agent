# How to Book an Appointment

## Overview

Most government services in Turkey require a prior appointment. You can book through the following channels.

---

## 1. Via e-Government Portal (turkiye.gov.tr)

1. Visit [turkiye.gov.tr](https://www.turkiye.gov.tr).
2. Log in with your Turkish ID Number (TC Kimlik No) and e-Government password.
3. Search for "randevu" (appointment) or navigate to the relevant service page.
4. Select the service type, office location, and an available date and time slot.
5. Confirm the appointment summary; you will receive an SMS and email confirmation.

**Required Information:**
- Turkish ID Number (TC Kimlik No)
- Registered mobile phone number or email address
- e-Government password (can be obtained at any PTT branch if you do not have one)

---

## 2. Via ALO 181 (Phone)

- **Line:** 181 (available 24/7; live operators available Mon–Fri, 08:00–20:00)
- Follow the automated menu to specify your service type and preferred office.
- An operator will offer you available time slots.
- Appointment details will be sent to your registered phone via SMS.

**Tip:** Wait times are longer during peak hours (09:00–11:00). Calling around lunchtime (12:30–13:30) often results in faster service.

---

## 3. Via Mobile App

- Download the "e-Devlet Kapisi" app or the relevant agency app from the App Store or Google Play.
- Log in with your TC ID and password to create an appointment.

---

## Services Available for Appointment Booking

| Service Type                  | Available Channels         |
|-------------------------------|----------------------------|
| National ID card / Passport   | e-Gov portal, 181, mobile  |
| Driver's license transactions | e-Gov portal, 181          |
| Land registry (Tapu)          | e-Gov portal (Tapu web)    |
| Social Security (SGK)         | e-Gov portal, 181          |
| Tax office                    | e-Gov portal               |

---

## Important Notes

- Arrive at least **10 minutes before** your scheduled appointment time.
- Bring **original documents** (National ID card and any required paperwork).
- Walk-in applications may not be accepted; always check availability online first.
