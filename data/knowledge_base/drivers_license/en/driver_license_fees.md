# Driver's License Transactions - Fee Schedule (2026)

## General Information
The fees listed below are current for 2026 and are set under the Highway Traffic Law.
Payments can be made at the Traffic Registration Office, PTT branches, or through the e-Government portal.

---

## Driver's License Fee Table

| Transaction Type | Fee (TRY) |
|---|---|
| New driver's license issuance (Class B) | 1,250 TRY |
| New driver's license issuance (Class A) | 1,250 TRY |
| New driver's license issuance (Classes C, D, E) | 1,500 TRY |
| License renewal (10-year validity stamp) | 950 TRY |
| Re-issuance due to loss/theft | 1,100 TRY |
| License class upgrade | 850 TRY |
| International Driving Permit (IDP) | 700 TRY |
| License information update (name, address, etc.) | 450 TRY |
| Duplicate license (second copy) | 450 TRY |

---

## Driving School Fees (Average)

| Course Type | Estimated Fee Range (TRY) |
|---|---|
| Class B (passenger car) driving course | 18,000 - 25,000 TRY |
| Class A1/A2 (motorcycle) driving course | 14,000 - 18,000 TRY |
| Class C (truck) driving course | 22,000 - 30,000 TRY |
| Class D (bus) driving course | 24,000 - 32,000 TRY |

> Note: Driving school fees vary by school and city. The figures above reflect averages for Istanbul, Ankara, and Izmir.

|---|---|
| Theoretical exam (first attempt) | 350 TRY |
| Theoretical exam (retake) | 350 TRY |
| Practical driving exam (first attempt) | 500 TRY |
| Practical driving exam (retake) | 500 TRY |

---

## Payment Methods

- **e-Government portal (turkiye.gov.tr):** Online payment by credit/debit card
- **PTT branches:** Cash or card payment
- **Traffic Registration Office cashier:** Cash or card payment
- **Bank transfer:** According to the relevant tax office IBAN details

---

## Important Notes

- Fees are updated annually according to the revaluation rate.
- Citizens aged 65 and above receive a 20% discount on renewal transactions.
- No document fee is charged for license transactions related to individuals with disabilities.
- Student discounts are available and vary by driving school.
